What is the fix for the CVT shudder on a 2021 CR-V?

There are two manufacturer remedies on file for this symptom and they do not agree with each other. Which one applies to your car depends on when it was built.

The earlier bulletin prescribes a CVT control software update and authorizes no hardware work.20-042 The later one supersedes it and calls for torque converter replacement on vehicles built before June 2021, keeping the software remedy for anything newer.22-018 Owner reports also describe the software update failing to hold on 2019 and 2020 cars.forum

Contradiction Unresolved
TSB 20-042 2020-03-11

A CVT control software update resolves the shudder. No hardware replacement is authorized.

TSB 22-018 2022-06-02

The software update did not resolve it. The torque converter must be replaced below the VIN cutoff.

Why these conflict. 22-018 supersedes 20-042 and splits the remedy on a build date. Vehicles built before June 2021 move to hardware replacement; later ones stay on software. A 2021 CR-V can fall on either side.
