My CR-V brakes hard on its own with nothing ahead. The dealer says that's normal. Is there a fix?

Not yet for a 2021. Honda's fix for this braking stops at the 2019 model year, and your car sits inside an open federal investigation instead.

Honda Service Bulletin 26-091 is a free software update to the adaptive cruise control and collision mitigation braking, but it covers 2017 to 2019 CR-Vs only.MC-11035781 NHTSA's engineering analysis into inadvertent emergency braking covers 2017 to 2022 CR-Vs, is still open, and has no recall attached.EA24-002 Your dealer's answer is one NHTSA noted: many owners say dealers couldn't reproduce the braking or called it normal operation.PE22-003 A 2021 owner was told the same in 2025.ODI-11679500
